Our Story

A honeycomb of good mornings, mid-century charm and second cups of coffee.

Bee's Bakery Cafe was built around a simple idea: breakfast should feel like an occasion, whether you're fueling up for a day on the trail or catching up with old friends over pancakes. Our dining room mixes vintage sewing machines, sunburst clocks and a whole lot of honeycomb gold — a warm, unmistakably local backdrop for scratch-made food.
